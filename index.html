ThinkVolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ThinkVolt ("ThinkVolt", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the ThinkVolt mobile application.

By using ThinkVolt, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

ThinkVolt may collect information that is necessary to provide account, gameplay, multiplayer, cloud-saving, advertising, and security functionality.

1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a ThinkVolt account, we may process:

  • Email address
  • User ID / account identifier
  • Username or display name, where provided
  • Authentication information managed through Firebase Authentication
  • Information associated with Google Sign-In when you choose to sign in with Google

Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate users and manage their accounts. Firebase documentation states that Firebase Authentication can process information such as email addresses, passwords, user-agent information and IP addresses to provide authentication and security functions.

1.2 Game and Account Data

ThinkVolt may store game-related information associated with your account, such as gameplay progress, scores, points, usernames, match information, rewards, and other game data generated through your use of the application.

This information may be stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

1.3 Advertising and Device Information

ThinkVolt uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements where applicable.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • App and user interactions, such as ad views and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising and fraud prevention

Google Mobile Ads documentation states that certain information may be collected and shared by the SDK for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Advertising identifier collection may also be subject to Android device settings and applicable advertising controls.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing ThinkVolt accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing Google Sign-In functionality
  • Saving and synchronizing game progress
  • Providing multiplayer and gameplay functionality
  • Maintaining scores, rewards, usernames, and game-related information
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Supporting rewarded advertisements
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining and improving the security and reliability of the application

3. Services and Third-Party Providers

ThinkVolt uses third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Firebase

ThinkVolt uses Google Firebase services including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Firebase may process information on our behalf to provide authentication, database, security, and related functionality.

Google Sign-In

ThinkVolt provides Google Sign-In as an authentication option. When you choose Google Sign-In, relevant account information is processed by Google to authenticate your account.

Google AdMob

ThinkVolt uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process device and advertising-related information to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, and Google AdMob when required to provide authentication, cloud storage, advertising, security, and other application functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of users, the application, or others.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through ThinkVolt. Firebase services use encrypted connections for data transmitted between the application and Firebase services.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Account and game-related information may be retained while your ThinkVolt account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the requested services.

Certain information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your ThinkVolt account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

When a valid account deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete the account and associated personal information from systems under our control, subject to information that must be retained to comply with legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations.

If ThinkVolt provides an in-app account deletion option, users may also use that option to request deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

ThinkVolt is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Permissions

ThinkVolt does not request access to your device's precise or approximate location, camera, or microphone for the functionality covered by this policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ThinkVolt, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
